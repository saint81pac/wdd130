Rapids! Make Memories!

We are a family-owned rafting company with experienced guides. Our goal is to help every guest enjoy a memorable river adventure safely.

History

Rapids! began in 1995 with two rafts and a love of the outdoors. Starting on local river stretches, we built a reputation for safety, teamwork, and welcoming first-time paddlers.

Today, we welcome families, friends, and teams from around the world to enjoy scenic floats, overnight riverside camps, and exciting white water runs with our experienced guides.
