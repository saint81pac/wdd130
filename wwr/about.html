Rapids! Make Memories!

We are a family-owned rafting company with experienced guides. Our goal is to help every guest enjoy a memorable river adventure safely.

History

Rapids! began in 1995 with two rafts and a love of the outdoors. Today, we welcome families, friends, and teams to enjoy the river with our experienced guides.

Adventure Awaits You!

Our Guides

Our experienced rafting guides lead each outing, teach paddling basics, and help guests stay safe on the river.

River Floats

Rapids
